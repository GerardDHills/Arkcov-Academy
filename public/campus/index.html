      The Cadet Corps

      Arkcov Academy is a real cadet program for students in middle school through high school. Every cadet starts with the Social Superpower Assessment, which reveals the strength they bring to medicine. Membership in the Academy unlocks how to use it.

      Junior Cadets

      Grades 6–8. Body foundations, exploration of healthcare careers, wellness and teamwork.

      Senior Cadets

      Grades 9–12. Advanced science, research, leadership, service and college preparation.

      Become a cadet

      Uniforms of the Corps

      Navy, crimson, cream and gold, from ceremony to the field.

      Four houses, one academy

      Junior Cadets live in the Boys' and Girls' Houses of the middle school quadrant; Senior Cadets in the Men's and Women's Houses. Each has hive-style sleep pods, study lounges and on-site academic and wellness support. Every house is single-sex.

      From screen to campus

      The campus begins as a virtual world, with virtual classrooms inside its halls. The goal is to make it real within the next 5 to 10 years: a place where young people train to become healthcare professionals.
